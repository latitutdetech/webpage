Chromebook management

FinTrak

Manage the day-to-day life of a school Chromebook, from student checkout to repairs and loaner returns.

  • Checkouts and loaners Record student assignments and returns, see which loaners are available, and track charger and insurance status.
  • Device history Look up a Chromebook’s assignment history, notes, and work orders alongside its Google Admin details, including organizational unit and last sync.
  • Inventory reports Find devices that are inactive, checked out, or still waiting to be issued. Review insurance by school year.

When a student brings in a broken Chromebook, look up the assigned device, record the repair, and check out a loaner.

School transportation

FinTransit

Give school staff and transportation teams a shared place to coordinate trips, vehicles, drivers, and schedules.

  • Trip coordination Keep trip details and transportation schedules together so staff can see what’s coming up.
  • Vehicle and driver assignments Reserve vehicles and assign drivers for school trips.
  • Staff notifications Send updates about assignments, bus keys, and departures.

For an away game, keep the trip, bus reservation, and driver assignment together so the people involved can see the plan.

School history

FinLegacy

Bring class composites, athletic records, and school photos together in a browsable archive for a hallway kiosk or the web.

  • Class composites Organize graduating class photos by year so visitors can find a class without searching the hallway.
  • Athletics history Collect team photos, records, and achievements so families and alumni can explore past seasons.
  • Community contributions Invite alumni and community members to share photos, missing records, and alumni profiles as the archive grows.

At a reunion, alumni can pull up their graduating class on the kiosk and browse school athletics history.
